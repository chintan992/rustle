# Rustle 1.1.1 — Privacy Policy

## Release notes

Rustle 1.1.1 adds a public Privacy Policy and a direct link to it from Settings.
The policy explains local book storage, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics,
opt-in announcement notifications, and model/update network requests.

## Play Store

- Version name: `1.1.1`
- Version code: `3`
- Upload the signed App Bundle at `app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`.
- Privacy Policy URL: <https://chintan992.github.io/rustle/privacy-policy/>
