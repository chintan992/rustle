# Rustle 1.2.0 — Guided Onboarding

Rustle 1.2.0 makes first launch a guided setup:

- Choose whether to allow announcement notifications when prompted. On Android
  13 and later, Android controls the notification permission; declining it does
  not block setup.
- Download and verify the Supertonic voice pack, then initialize the speech
  engine before continuing.
- Choose a default narrator, then arrive at the library.
- Resume setup at the appropriate step if the app closes during model setup.

Existing users who have already completed onboarding continue to open the
library as before. This release also updates the first-run instructions.

Requires Android 7.0 (API 24) or later. The voice pack download requires an
internet connection and about 138 MB of storage. Narration works offline after
the pack is installed.

Choose the APK matching your device ABI. The in-app updater uses the
`rustle-update.json` file attached to this release.
