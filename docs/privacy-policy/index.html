Rustle Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains how the Rustle Android app handles information. Rustle is published by Chintan (the “developer,” “I,” or “we”).

Information stored on your device

Rustle does not require an account. Books and documents you import, extracted text, generated narration audio, bookmarks, playback position, listening statistics, and app preferences are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Rustle does not upload your book content, file names, or generated audio to the developer.

Android device backup may include small app settings and reading sidecars, depending on your Android backup settings. Book and voice-model files are excluded from Rustle’s Android backup rules. Backup data, when enabled by you at the device level, is handled by your Android backup provider and is subject to that provider’s terms and privacy policy.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

Rustle uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app usage and diagnose crashes. These services may collect app interaction and lifecycle events, app and device information, Firebase/app-instance identifiers, and crash reports or stack traces. Rustle’s custom analytics events are intended to contain limited technical values (such as language or voice-pack identifiers), not book titles, file names, text, or audio. The Firebase SDK may also collect information automatically as described in Google’s Firebase documentation. Analytics and crash reporting are enabled by default; Rustle currently has no in-app setting to turn them off.

This information is sent to and processed by Google as Rustle’s analytics and crash-reporting service provider. Google may process it on servers in other countries under its terms and privacy practices. Rustle does not use this information for advertising or sell it. Google’s privacy information is available at Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy and security.

Optional announcement notifications

If you opt in to announcements, Rustle uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). FCM processes an app/device registration token and the subscription needed to deliver messages on the Rustle announcements topic. Google processes this information to provide push messaging. You can turn announcements off in Rustle Settings; Android notification permission can also be denied or revoked in system settings. Rustle disables FCM auto-initialization until you enable announcements.

Network requests

Rustle connects to Hugging Face to download voice-model files when you choose to install a model, and to GitHub to check for app updates when you request an update check. Those services receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and request metadata, as necessary to deliver the requested files or update information. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies: Hugging Face and GitHub.

Speech synthesis is performed on your device. An internet connection is not needed to narrate books after the voice model is installed.

Retention and your choices

Local app data remains on your device until you clear Rustle’s storage or uninstall Rustle. Android backup copies, if enabled, are managed by your backup provider. Google manages retention for Firebase data under its service settings and policies. You can control announcement delivery in Settings and Android notification settings. Android and Google may provide controls for some device identifiers and analytics data. You may contact the developer about Firebase data associated with your app use; deletion requests will be handled where the relevant service permits.

Children

Rustle is a general-purpose audiobook reader and is not designed specifically for children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
